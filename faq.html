Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about Rentora tenancy, escrow security, property listings, and automated owner payouts.

Updated: October 2026 Category: Tenancy & Operations Average Response: Under 15 mins
What is Rentora and how does it simplify renting?
Rentora is a premier property management platform engineered for property owners and discerning tenants. We manage end-to-end leasing operations including photographic verified listings, KYC background screening, digital biometric lease stamping, scheduled rent collection, and 24/7 dedicated maintenance dispatch.
How are security deposits and escrow funds protected?
All tenant security deposits and advance rentals are held in RBI-regulated scheduled bank escrow accounts (ICICI Bank Trustee custody). Funds are only settled upon joint digital sign-off and move-out condition audits, eliminating return delays and arbitrary deductions.
How do I schedule an in-person or live video property visit?
You can book guided visits directly from any property page on our website or contact our concierge desk. Our dedicated relationship managers coordinate security passes and personally escort you through the property. Live 4K interactive video walkthroughs are also available for remote relocation tenants.
What is the standard lease duration and notice period?
Standard residential tenancies are executed for 11 months under legally validated digital e-stamps with a standard 1-month written notice period. Multi-year corporate leases with agreed annual escalation rates can also be structured on request.
How does Rentora handle maintenance and emergency repairs?
Tenants can submit service tickets directly via the Resident Dashboard with photos and video notes. Routine maintenance is attended to within 24 hours. Emergency issues (e.g. plumbing pipe bursts, power cutoffs) trigger instant dispatch with verified technicians on-site within 90 minutes.
Do you offer remote property care for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)?
Yes. Over 40% of our managed portfolios belong to NRI homeowners worldwide. We manage key handover, high-yield tenant placement, municipal property tax settlements, routine condition reporting, and automated rent remittance to NRE/NRO accounts.
