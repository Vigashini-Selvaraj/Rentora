GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Rentora services, fees, security, and onboarding.

Rentora operates as a direct digital platform connecting verified property owners directly with pre-screened tenants. We eliminate traditional middlemen and brokers, saving owners up to 1 month's rent in commissions and giving tenants 100% brokerage-free access.

Every prospective resident undergoes government ID verification, employment and income checks, previous landlord references, and optional civil background screening before a lease agreement is executed.

Rent invoices are automatically issued on the 1st of every month via WhatsApp and email. Tenants pay securely via UPI, NetBanking, or debit/credit cards. Funds are deposited directly into the owner's bank account with real-time ledger updates.

Tenants can log maintenance requests with photos via the Rentora portal. Our coordinator assigns a pre-vetted local technician within 24 hours. Owners review and approve cost estimates before any work above a pre-set threshold begins.

Onboarding takes less than 15 minutes. You can upload property photos, specify rental terms and amenities, and request our free verification photographer. Once verified, your property goes live immediately.
