DATA PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy.

We treat your tenancy records, financial documents, and personal details with the highest standards of data protection.

Last Updated: October 2026 Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Security: 256-bit AES Vault Encryption

01 Information We Collect

To deliver seamless rental leasing, identity verification, and escrow account administration, we collect:

  • Identity & KYC Details: Full legal name, official email address, mobile number, government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport) for tenant screening.
  • Property & Ownership Records: Title deeds, municipal tax receipts, and electricity consumer account numbers from property owners.
  • Transaction Records: Rent remittance logs, security deposit receipts, and bank account information for automated payouts.

02 How We Use Your Data

We use collected information solely for legitimate rental property management functions:

  • Verifying tenant creditworthiness, employment, and rental background.
  • Executing digital legally stamped lease agreements via licensed e-stamp channels.
  • Processing maintenance requests and dispatching background-checked technicians.
  • Preventing fraud and securing deposit escrow fund releases.
Zero Data Selling Guarantee: Rentora never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data to third-party telemarketers or external advertisers.

03 Storage & Bank-Grade Security

All sensitive records are stored in encrypted databases hosted in ISO 27001 certified data centers located within India in compliance with RBI data localization mandates. Data in transit is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption, and resting documents are protected by AES-256 vault encryption.

04 Your Rights & Data Retention

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to inspect, correct, or request the deletion of your personal profile upon the successful conclusion and clearance of your active lease tenure. Tax and lease execution copies are retained for the statutory period required by Indian real estate law.

05 Data Protection Officer (DPO)

For any queries or formal data requests, contact our Data Privacy Officer at privacy@rentora.in or write to Rentora Experience Center, Level 4, Prestige Tower, Anna Salai, Chennai 600002.
