OPERATIONAL SUMMARY

Actionable playbook for property owners.

Three core operating principles distilled from this guide that you can immediately apply to your portfolio.

STAGE 01

Complete Pre-Inspection

Audit electrical safety, water pressure, window hinges, and wall surfaces 14 days before move-in to eliminate move-in punch lists.

STAGE 02

Standardized Handover Kit

Provide tenants with appliance instruction manuals, building association by-laws, and emergency plumber contact cards in a single folder.

STAGE 03

Digital Milestone Tracking

Automate reminder calendars for 60-day lease renewal reviews, annual AC servicing, and security deposit escrow reconciliations.

EXPERT Q&A

Common questions & expert advice.

Specific scenarios encountered by landlords and property managers regarding this topic.

How far in advance should property preparations begin?

We recommend starting preparation 15 to 20 days prior to the new tenancy. This leaves adequate buffer time for vendor repairs, professional deep-cleaning, and touch-up paint drying without delaying the tenant's move-in schedule.

What documentation is essential during the handover meeting?

Always complete a signed photographic inventory report, utility meter reading logs (electricity and water), key count acknowledgment, and registered emergency contact confirmations.
