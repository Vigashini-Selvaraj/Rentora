LEGAL GOVERNANCE

Terms & Policy.

Standard operating terms governing residential leasing contracts, security deposits, escrow accounts, and tenant responsibilities.

Effective Date: 01 October 2026 Jurisdiction: Tamil Nadu, India Version: 3.2

01 Platform Scope & Agency Relationship

Rentora acts as an authorized professional property management facilitator connecting verified property owners ("Landlords") with verified occupants ("Tenants"). By creating an account or browsing properties on Rentora, you agree to comply with all applicable tenancy laws and digital contract guidelines.

Rentora is not a broker of unverified listings. All residences listed on Rentora have passed title verification and in-person photographic inspection.

02 Tenancy Agreements & Digital E-Stamping

All rental contracts initiated via Rentora are registered under legally binding digital e-stamping protocols through National E-Governance Services (NeSL) and state stamp duty authorities. Both parties agree that digital signatures executed via Aadhaar OTP or approved electronic certificate hold full evidentiary weight.

03 Security Deposits & Escrow Management

Tenant security deposits (typically 2 to 3 months' agreed rent) are deposited into scheduled bank escrow accounts maintained under regulated trust terms. Escrow releases require:

  • Formal completion of the 11-month lease term or approved early surrender notice.
  • Digital joint handover checklist sign-off by Rentora property inspectors and tenant.
  • Settlement of outstanding utility, electricity (TNEB), and verified repair bills.

04 Rent Collection & Payment Timelines

Monthly rentals are due on or before the 5th day of every calendar month through the Rentora Resident Portal via UPI, IMPS, or credit card. Late remittances beyond a 5-day grace period may incur a statutory administrative fee of 1.5% per month until settled.

05 Code of Conduct & Maintenance Access

Tenants agree to maintain properties in reasonable repair, observe community society bye-laws, and provide access to scheduled technicians with 24 hours prior digital notification, except in urgent emergencies where prompt mitigating action is required.

06 Termination & Disputes

Either party may terminate the tenancy by delivering 30 days' formal written notice via the Rentora portal. Any disputes arising out of the agreement will be referred to mediation in Chennai in accordance with the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act.
